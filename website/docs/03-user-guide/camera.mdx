# Camera

AgeLapse includes a built-in camera designed for consistent photo capture. Use it to take perfectly aligned photos every time.

## Camera Controls

On **mobile devices** (iOS/Android), you'll see these controls at the bottom of the camera screen:

| Control | Location | Function |
|---------|----------|----------|
| **Flash** | Bottom-left | Toggle between Auto and Off |
| **Camera Switch** | Bottom-right | Switch between front and rear camera |

:::info
Flash and camera switching are only available on mobile. Desktop uses your system camera settings.
:::

## Overlay Modes

Tap the **grid icon** in the camera view to cycle through overlay modes:

### None
Clean viewfinder with no overlay. Use when you're confident in your positioning.

### Ghost
Shows a faint (30% opacity) overlay of your first stabilized photo. Align your face with the ghost image to match your previous position.

### Grid
Shows guide lines only:
- **Two vertical lines** mark where your eyes should be
- **One horizontal line** marks the eye height

### Ghost + Grid
Combines both the ghost image and guide lines for maximum precision.

:::tip
**Ghost + Grid** mode gives you the best alignment accuracy. Use it when getting started or when precise positioning matters.
:::

## Customizing Guide Position

You can move the guide lines to better match your preferred framing:

1. Switch to **Grid** or **Ghost + Grid** mode
2. Tap **Move Guides** (appears at bottom-left)
3. Drag the lines to your preferred position:
   - Drag vertical lines left/right to adjust eye spacing
   - Drag the horizontal line up/down to adjust eye height
4. Tap the **checkmark** to save

:::info
Camera guide positions are separate from output position settings. Adjusting guides here changes where you align in the viewfinder, not where eyes appear in the final video. [Adjust output position →](./tutorials/update-output-position)
:::

## Tips for Consistent Photos

1. **Use the same camera** - Front camera is recommended for selfies
2. **Match the ghost image** - Align your eyes with the overlay
3. **Keep consistent lighting** - Same time of day helps
4. **Same expression** - Neutral face works best for alignment
5. **Hold steady** - The camera countdown gives you time to settle
