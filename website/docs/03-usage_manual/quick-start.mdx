<div className={"tutorial"}>

<div class="step">
    ## <div class="step-circle">1</div><span class="step-text">Download and Install</span>
</div>

<div>
    a. Go to <a target={"_blank"} href={"https://agelapse.com"}>agelapse.com</a><br/>
    b. Select the applicable download for your device<br/>
    c. Install and open the <b>AgeLapse</b> app
</div>

<div class="step">
    ## <div class="step-circle">2</div><span class="step-text">Create Your First Project</span>
</div>

<div>
    a. Tap <b>Create New Project</b><br/>
    b. Enter a project name<br/>
    c. Tap <b>Create</b>
</div>

<div class="step">
    ## <div class="step-circle">3</div><span class="step-text">Import Photos</span>
</div>

<div>
    a. Tap <b>Import</b> (top-right in the gallery). Need a walkthrough? Open <b>Info → Tutorials → Import photos</b><br/>
    b. Select:<br/>
    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• <b>ZIP archive</b> <i>(recommended)</i><br/>
    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• <b>Gallery</b> or <b>File System</b> (AgeLapse supports: .png, .jpg, .jpeg, .webp, .heic, .avif)<br/>
    c. Wait for the import to finish – photos will be auto-sorted by date
</div>

:::tip
For large batches (50+), zip them on your PC first to preserve metadata and speed up import.
:::

<div class="step">
    ## <div class="step-circle">4</div><span class="step-text">Stabilize Your Photos</span>
</div>

<div>
    a. Switch to the <b>Stabilize</b> tab<br/>
    b. Watch your photos align and stabilize in real time<br/>
    c. Sit back and relax while AgeLapse handles everything automatically
</div>

:::info
If using a mobile device, you may have to leave your app open during stabilization. This is due to restrictions imposed by your phone. It'll be worth it, we promise!
:::

<div class="step">
    ## <div class="step-circle">5</div><span class="step-text">Preview and Customize</span>
</div>

<div>
    a. After stabilization, tap <b>Play Video</b> in the bottom navigation bar<br/>
    b. Adjust playback speed using the <b>Speed</b> control<br/>
    c. Open <b>Settings</b> to customize output:<br/>
    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• <b>Eye Position</b> (inner eye distance, vertical offset)<br/>
    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• <b>Frame Rate</b><br/>
    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• <b>Orientation</b><br/>
    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• <b>Resolution</b><br/>
    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• <b>Aspect Ratio</b>
</div>

<div class="step">
    ## <div class="step-circle">6</div><span class="step-text">Advanced Features</span>
</div>

<div>
    a. <b>Live Stabilization</b>: Any photo you take in-app (front/back camera) auto-aligns<br/>
    b. <b>Multiple Projects</b>: Manage several projects side by side<br/>
    c. <b>Reminders</b>: Set project reminders to keep you on track
</div>

</div>
