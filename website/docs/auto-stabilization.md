title: Automatic Photo Stabilization
---

## 1. Create a New Project
- Open AgeLapse and tap **Create Project**.
- Enter a descriptive name and confirm.

## 2. Import Your Photos
- Tap **Import** and select a ZIP archive or folder of images.
- Wait for the import to complete; thumbnails will appear in the gallery.

## 3. Stabilize Automatically
- Switch to the **Stabilize** tab.
- Your photos will align in real time—just sit back and watch the progress bar.
- Once finished, click **Play Video** to preview your stabilized time‑lapse.

## 4. Customize Output
You can adjust:
- **Eye Position** (horizontal & vertical offsets)
- **Playback Speed**
- **Resolution & Aspect Ratio**
- **Frame Rate & Orientation**

All settings are available under **App Settings** > **Project Settings**.
